# Cancellations Policy

> Demo policy for Northstar Market, a fictional e-commerce company.

An order may be cancelled only while its status is `processing`. Contact support with the Northstar Market order ID as soon as possible. Cancellation is not guaranteed until support confirms it.

Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. After delivery, an eligible item may be returned under the Returns Policy. Partial cancellation of an order is not supported in this demo policy.

For an approved cancellation, Northstar Market submits the refund to the original payment method within 2 business days. The bank or card issuer may take an additional 5 to 10 business days to post it.
