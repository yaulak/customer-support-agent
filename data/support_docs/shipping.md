# Shipping Policy

> Demo policy for Northstar Market, a fictional e-commerce company.

Orders normally require 1 to 2 business days for processing. Business days are Monday through Friday and exclude public holidays. Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 5 business days after shipment, while expedited shipping normally takes 1 to 2 business days after shipment.

Delivery dates are estimates rather than guarantees. A tracking link is emailed when the carrier receives the package. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show its first update.

If tracking says delivered but the package cannot be found, check nearby delivery areas and contact support within 7 calendar days. Northstar Market will open a carrier investigation before offering a replacement or refund.
