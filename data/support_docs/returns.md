# Returns Policy

> Demo policy for Northstar Market, a fictional e-commerce company.

Most items may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with any tags attached. A valid Northstar Market order ID is required.

Final-sale items, personalized products, digital gift cards, and opened hygiene products cannot be returned. If an item arrived damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, contact support within 7 calendar days after delivery.

Customers are responsible for return shipping on change-of-mind returns. Northstar Market provides return shipping when an item is damaged, defective, or incorrect. Refund timing is described in the Refunds Policy.
