# Refunds Policy

> Demo policy for Northstar Market, a fictional e-commerce company.

Returned items are inspected within 3 business days after they reach Northstar Market. Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method within 2 business days after inspection. Banks and card issuers may take an additional 5 to 10 business days to post the refund.

Original shipping charges and change-of-mind return shipping costs are not refundable. When Northstar Market sent a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, the approved refund includes the original standard shipping charge.

Approved order cancellations are refunded to the original payment method. The same 5-to-10-business-day bank processing window applies after the refund is submitted.
